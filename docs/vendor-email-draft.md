# Vendor email: new Market Participant Directory

Draft for AIM to send to market participants with the intake form link, through the MP
newsletter or a separate email. Written in AIM's voice. Items in brackets need filling in.

---

**Subject:** A new way for shoppers to find you at AIM markets

Hi [first name],

We're building a new Market Participant Directory for the AIM website. It will help shoppers find your business, see which of our markets you're at and when, and check how they can pay at your booth.

**What the directory shows**

- From your Manage My Market profile: your business name, description, city, phone, website and social links, and your market dates.
- From a short new form: the payment types you accept and where you grow or make your products.
- It never shows your email or street address.

**Why we're asking about payment types**

Shoppers and market managers have asked us for a simple way to see which booths accept EBT, WIC, SFMNB and cards. Manage My Market doesn't hold that information, so we're collecting it directly from you.

**One quick ask**

Please fill out this two-minute form by [date]: [form link]

**Sharing your information**

The directory only includes businesses that checked the box in Manage My Market allowing AIM to share their information publicly. If you've already checked it, there's nothing more to do. If you haven't, or you'd like to change your answer, you can update it in your Manage My Market profile at any time.

Questions? Just reply to this email.

Thank you,

[name]
